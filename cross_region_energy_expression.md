two coherence potentials — Φ_stability and Φ_relational — that

## Two distinct coherence potentials

### Φ_stability[v] = ι[v] · τ_stability[v]

τ_stability[v] is the directly observable persistence interval —
the decay lifetime of the locus. Directly measurable through M.

Φ_stability[v] measures how much relational structure a locus
sustains over its lifetime. Confirmed across 29 declared loci
spanning 78 decades. Basis for the stable threshold, the locus
ordering, and the coherence accumulation findings.

Observable source: PDG 2024, NIST ASD v5.12.
Support: Observed. Confirmed in validation_record_v2.md VR-Φ-01.

### Φ_relational[v] = ι[v] · τ_relational[v] = ι[v] · ℏ / E[v]

τ_relational[v] = ℏ / E[v] is the relational progression interval
produced by the operator framework — the isolated-locus limit of
the unified τ expression (OC-13 resolved). It is not the decay
lifetime. It is the inverse of the relational field magnitude.

Φ_relational[v] measures the action quantum per unit relational
field magnitude at the declared locus.

ι[v] — relational inertia — replaces m[v] throughout this document.
Rest mass declared inadmissible (SF-PR-16). ι[v] is the inertial
response of a declared locus under the declared non-acceleration
condition through M. Numerical values unchanged. Within the
declared framework, the observable admitted through M is
represented by ι[v]; the numerical values carry forward from
the measurement record; the primitive interpretation changes.

At the Primary Region, for all declared loci:
Φ_relational = ℏ / κ[Primary]

where κ[Primary] = 8.988×10¹⁶ J/kg is the confirmed regional
energy scale (see below). This is constant for all Primary Region
loci — independent of mass or lifetime.

Support: Derived — from τ_relational = ℏ/E[v] (unified τ
expression, OC-13 resolved) and Φ = ι · τ.

## The cross-region energy expression

**E[v] · Φ_relational[v] = ι[v] · ℏ**

Equivalently: **E[v] = ι[v] · κ[region]**

where **κ[region] = ℏ / Φ_relational[region]**

κ[region] is the **regional energy scale** — the energy per unit
relational inertia at the declared region's operator scale.
Units: J/kg. κ[region] is not imported from any classical
framework. It is the ratio of the declared action reference to
the relational coherence scale at that region.

The form E[v] = ι[v] · κ[region] is invariant across all
declared regions. What changes across regions is κ[region] —
the declared energy scale of that region's operator application.

**Support classification:** Derived — algebraic identity from
the isolated-locus limit of the unified τ expression (OC-13)
and the definition of Φ_relational.

## What energy is in the framework

Energy is not a primitive. It is not directly observable through M.
What M reports is always one of:
- A frequency at a detection locus → ε[e] through declared mapping
- A mass deficit between declared loci → ε[e] binding energy
- A momentum at a declared locus → kinetic energy through declared mapping
- An instrument response → energy through declared calibration

In every case, energy is a Layer 3 derived reading of the
relational structure through a declared measurement mapping M.

No classical model quantities — potential energy, force,
temperature, entropy, Hamiltonian — enter D without independent
declaration through M. Published measurement values that were
produced using established theoretical apparatus are used as
declared observational references through M, not as theoretical
imports.

## Photon edges and κ[region]

### ι[photon] = 0 — the photon locus is not an inertial locus

The cross-region energy expression E[v] = ι[v] · κ[region] holds for
declared inertial loci: loci whose inertial response is traceable through
M under the declared non-acceleration condition (A_persistence = 0 at
the measurement edge). The photon locus does not satisfy this condition.
No inertial response of the photon is traceable through M. ι[photon] = 0
by declaration.

Consequence: the form E[v] = ι[v] · κ[region] does not apply at the
photon locus. κ[region] is not computable from photon edge data alone.
The photon edge energy ε[e] = hc/λ enters D through the M mapping from
NIST ASD v5.12; it is an edge quantity, not a node quantity of the form
ι · κ. No c² identity arises from photon edge data.

### Photon edges as a clean Atomic Region observable path

Because ι[photon] = 0, photon edge data does not generate a κ[Atomic]
candidate. The 14 confirmed Atomic Region photon edge transitions
(VR-γ-01, validation_record_v4.md) extend the observable record at the
Atomic Region without touching the κ[Primary] = c² admissibility question.
ε[e], τ[v], and N[v] are all confirmed from photon edge data; their
provenance is clean and independent of the κ · ι = E identity.

### Establishing κ[Atomic] still requires ι[emitting locus]

To establish κ[Atomic] = ε[e] / ι[emitting locus] at the Atomic Region,
the emitting locus must be declared as an inertial locus with ι[v]
declared through M (mass spectrometry under declared non-acceleration
condition). The M mapping for Atomic Region inertia has not yet been
formally declared in this context. κ[Atomic] remains open program (OC-E-2).

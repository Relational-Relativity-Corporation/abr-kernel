# The Operators — Notation and Constraint

**Metatron Dynamics, Inc.** Kernel V8. Reference data. Bounded over D. No claim beyond D.

**Admissibility.** A statement is admissible within D if and only if every quantity in it can be traced to an observable through M. Declarability is necessary but not sufficient. A quantity that is declared but not traceable to an observable through M is not inadmissible — it is not about anything in D. Declaration is never a substitute for traceability to an observable through M. Declaration is the act of stating traceability to an observable through M and taking responsibility for it. The admissibility condition is not relaxed by the act of declaration — it is what every declaration is required to satisfy.

**Domain.** D := { x ∈ ℝⁿ | n < ∞, |x[i]| < ∞ }. O → M → D. M : O → D, declared before anything is taken. A value in D is the numerical projection of an observable through M. The kernel acts on M(o) only. Operator outputs are calculated results; a calculated result may be compared with observation and remains a calculated result. No numerical result is itself an observable. *Declared information:* the kernel makes no declaration about what exists or does not exist. Where information required by an operator has not been declared through M for the calculation, that operator is not evaluated. No substitute value or classification is introduced. A 0.0 produced by an operator over declared information is a calculated result and is returned as such. *Sequential observation requirement:* Phase 1 (spatial) is admissible on a single declared observation M(o). Phase 2 (persistence) requires M to declare a sequence {M(o₁), M(o₂), ..., M(oₙ)} where each oₖ is a declared observation at one relational step and the relational step is the system's own declared process step — not an externally imposed increment and not a model-generated value. A model-generated trajectory is not an observable sequence through M; it is a formal construction that may be declared within M only when the model is itself declared as a transducer from observable inputs. *Persistence:* persistence requires a declared pair of sequential observations. At the first declared observation, only the spatial kernel is evaluated. A_persistence, B_persistence, and R_persistence are evaluated over (E_{n−1}, E_n) once both have been declared through M. No prior value is supplied where no prior observation was declared.

**Relations.** A declared relation is either: (1) a directed spatial edge e = (s, t) where s and t are node-valued loci; or (2) a directed persistence relation connecting two complete kernel output states E_prior[e] and E_current[e] across one declared relational step, where the loci are edge-valued. *Constraint:* every relation must be traceable to an observable through M. A relation that cannot be traced to an observable through M is fabricated within D, even when it computes. There is no provenance source other than the observable. Declaration is always a declaration of traceability to an observable through M, never a substitute for it. A relation is declared through M from an observed distinction. Δ and A compute the numerical directed difference over a declared relation; the computation does not declare the relation.

**Declaration correspondence.** Analysis proceeds from declared observables without expectation of numerical outcome. Relations, variables, and topology are declared through M; operators act only on those declared numerical projections and return their results unchanged. Observable → M → numerical projection in D → declared topology → operators → calculated result → comparison with observables. Where the numerical projection produced by Δ or A does not preserve the observed distinction on which a declared relation is based, correspondence is not established; the declaration may be varied and evaluated again — topology first, with variable determination and M. The kernel does not vary the declaration. Topology is varied in response to comparison with observables, not to obtain a particular numerical result. No interpretation beyond the declared observable correspondence follows from calculation alone.

**Relational direction.** Every declared relation has exactly one admissible direction — the direction traceable to an observable through M. This holds without distinction for spatial relations, persistence relations, and hadronic transitions. The reverse direction requires independent observable provenance. If it does not have it, it is inadmissible. *Distinctness axiom (declared condition):* if both (s,t) and (t,s) are independently observed and declared, they are treated as distinct relations with distinct provenance — not as reverse observations of the same relation. Whether this axiom holds for a specific observable is a question for the declaring party at declaration time. *Consequence:* within the declared admissibility conditions, a symmetric declared edge-image signals inadmissible structure — a reverse edge declared without satisfying the distinctness axiom and independent provenance requirement. Ring topology is inadmissible: the closing edge declares a node as its own relational predecessor through a chain; within the declared admissibility conditions, no quantity has itself as a relational predecessor, as such a quantity requires solving a fixed-point equation to have a definite value, which is not traceable to an observable through M.

**Relational evolution direction.** The ordering of the declared sequence {M(o₁), M(o₂), ..., M(oₙ)} has exactly one admissible direction — the direction the process itself moves, traceable to an observable through M. This direction is a property of the process, not of the index. Indexing the sequence 1, 2, ..., n does not declare the direction; it assigns labels. The direction is declared when Origin identifies what observable property of the process establishes that oₖ₊₁ follows oₖ — for example: an oscillating system moves from floor through expression to recovery, establishing one declared process step in one direction; energy flows from a higher-relational-contrast configuration toward a lower one; a conformational change proceeds from one declared state toward another; a decaying system moves irreversibly from one configuration toward its terminal state. These are all declared process steps. None require recurrence or periodicity. Temporal ordering is not a primitive declaration — it is a downstream projection of relational evolution onto a one-dimensional axis. Time does not establish direction; the process establishes direction, and time is one way of labeling the steps of that process after the direction has been identified from the observable. *Constraint:* the direction of relational evolution must be declared by identifying the observable property of the process that establishes which observation is prior and which is current. Index order alone is not an admissible direction declaration. A declared direction of relational evolution that cannot be traced to an observable property of the process is fabricated within D.

**Composition.** V6 defines two kernels. *Primary kernel* (V6, new): E_primary(x) = Σ(Δ(x)) over spatial relations at the Primary Region — where the mechanism of the declared relational structure is not yet resolvable by M. *ABR kernel* (V5, unchanged): Phase 1 produces E_spatial = R(B(A(x)), ρ(A(x))) over spatial relations; Phase 2 produces A_persistence → B_persistence → R_persistence over persistence relations using the same operator formulas over edge-valued loci. The order A → B → R is fixed in both phases. C is a declared projection, not a kernel operator. The kernel boundary is determined by the full-operator admissibility condition — see below.

**A — relational directed difference.** Spatial form: A(x)[e] = x[s] − x[t] over each declared spatial edge e = (s, t). Persistence form: A_persistence[e] = E_current[e] − E_prior[e] over each declared persistence relation — the directed difference between the current and prior complete kernel output at edge e. The formula is identical; the loci are edge-valued rather than node-valued. The direction E_current − E_prior is fixed by the observable: E_prior is the state before the declared relational step, E_current is the state after it. The reverse direction is not traceable to an observable through M. *Constraint:* take the directed difference and nothing else — add no relation and no direction the declaration did not trace to an observable through M. This holds for both forms. *Sequential constraint on persistence form:* A_persistence is evaluated only over a declared pair (E_prior, E_current), where E_prior is the kernel output from the preceding declared observation in the sequence {M(o₁), M(o₂), ...} — not from a model, a parameter value, or a repeated static configuration. At the first declared observation A_persistence is not evaluated; no prior value is supplied. A_persistence over a static geometry — where E_current = E_prior because M(o) does not change — produces 0.0 at every edge. This is a calculated result over declared information and is returned as such: no relational evolution occurred across that declared step. *Correspondence:* every A or Δ result, including 0.0, is returned unchanged — not deleted, thresholded, or reclassified. Where a declared relation's projection does not preserve the observed distinction on which it is based, correspondence is examined per the declaration correspondence entry above.

**Δ — primary directed difference.** Δ is A under a declared renaming, identifying its role as the irreducible primitive of the primary kernel. Δ(x)[e] = x[s] − x[t] over each declared spatial edge. Formula identical to A. Used in the primary kernel where B is not active.

**B — local accumulation.** B(g)[e] = g[e] + Σ_{f ∈ succ(e)} g[f], same direction only. *Accumulation depth:* succ(e) is the set of immediate declared successors of e. B accumulates over immediate successors only; it does not accumulate transitively along continuation chains, and it is not evaluated in reverse topological order. B(g)[e] depends on g, never on B(g) at another edge. *Constraint:* accumulate only along declared continuation. A terminal edge accumulates nothing; no boundary is closed to supply one. B is not evaluated in the primary kernel — it is not an identity operator evaluated with no effect. Not evaluated ≠ evaluated with result 0.0. B is evaluated when the full-operator admissibility condition (below) holds.

**R — antisymmetric circulation.** R(g)[e] = g[e] + ρ[e]·( Σ_{succ(e)} g − Σ_{pred(e)} g ); where spatial and component relations cross, the asymmetry of each enters the other, scaled by cc[p], the cross-topology coupling declared by M for each declared component pair p. cc[p] has no kernel value and no default; with no component pairs declared, the cross-topology term is not evaluated. *Open (A-3):* sums over an empty declared adjacency set evaluate to 0.0 in R and Σ. Whether these sums are defined over the set of declared adjacent relations, so that an empty set contributes nothing, is to be established from the operator declaration. Not classified. *Constraint:* couple by the asymmetry present. Under the declared admissibility conditions, every admissible declared structure is asymmetric — symmetry requires independent provenance per direction under the distinctness axiom, in which case both directions are distinct relations, not a symmetric pair. Do not assume the two directions equal without declared independent provenance for each.

**Σ — primary antisymmetric circulation.** Σ is R applied directly to Δ(x) without prior B accumulation. Σ(g)[e] = g[e] + ρ[e]·( Σ_{adj⁺(e)} g − Σ_{adj⁻(e)} g ), where g = Δ(x). adj⁺(e): declared edges whose source = target of e. adj⁻(e): declared edges whose target = source of e. Adjacency sums are over immediate neighbors only. Σ is R at the Primary Region limit — the same formula, the same operator, a different input condition. Not a new operator.

**ρ — local strength.** *Node form:* ρ[i] = ρ_base · χ[i] / (χ₀ + χ[i]), with χ[i] the largest absolute directed difference at node i — the maximum of |A(x)[e]| or |Δ(x)[e]| over all edges incident to i. χ[i] replaces m[i] throughout (SF-PR-16 retired m[v] as a name for relational inertia; χ[i] is the distinct local asymmetry magnitude used in ρ, not a relational inertia quantity). *Constraint:* derived per node from A(x) or Δ(x); no aggregation beyond the node. *Edge form:* ρ[e] := ρ[source(e)] — the node-form quantity evaluated at the source locus of the declared edge. *Preserves:* the source locus strength. *Discards:* the target locus strength. The asymmetry is declared, not incidental, and is consistent with the single admissible direction of the relation. *Constraint:* the edge form is a declared evaluation of the node form at a stated locus, not a second definition; where an implementation returns one value per edge it is returning the edge form and must say so. *M-declared parameters:* ρ_base (dimensionless) and χ₀ (in the dimension of χ, declared through M) are supplied by M for each analysis. The kernel holds no value for either and supplies no default. χ₀ must be finite and positive for the map to be evaluable. Where they are not declared by M, the operator is not evaluated and there is no operator result. For declared ρ_base > 0, ρ ∈ [0, ρ_base) in both forms. *Open:* the max selection rule for χ[i] and the saturating form χ/(χ₀+χ) each require explicit Origin declaration.

**C — declared projection.** Any reduction of the edge field — to one value per node, to a bound, to σ². *Constraint:* state what it preserves and what it discards. No silent reduction. C is not a kernel operator in either kernel.

**Before A / Before Δ.** *Constraint:* no transformation that alters pairwise differences precedes A or Δ unless declared within M. Admissible: uniform shift, declared unit scale. All else requires declaration with preserved and discarded invariants stated.

**Observational resolution principle.** Let P denote a declared relational phenomenon — a property of the declared relational structure produced by the kernel operators from M(o).

ℛ_M(P) = 1: the observable record contains a traceable expression of P through M. The phenomenon is observationally grounded at the scale where M operates.

𝓜_M(P) = 1: M resolves the internal mechanism of P — the constituent loci and their directed relations are independently declarable through M with sufficient provenance to instantiate the relational structure explicitly.

𝓜_M(P) = 1 ⟹ ℛ_M(P) = 1. The converse does not hold in general.

*Observational status is scale-conditional:*

When ℛ_M(P) = 1 and 𝓜_M(P) = 0: the phenomenon is observed through M at the available scale; its internal mechanism is not yet resolvable by M. *Classification: expression-observed, mechanism-unresolved.*

When ℛ_M(P) = 1 and 𝓜_M(P) = 1: the phenomenon is observed and its internal mechanism is resolvable through M. *Classification: locus-resolved.*

The declared relational phenomenon P is region-invariant — the same operators acting on the same declared relational structure produce the same phenomenon at every region where it is operative. What is region-dependent is 𝓜_M(P): whether the measurement apparatus available at that region can independently resolve the constituent loci and their directed relations. The claim that P differs between regions requires independent observable grounding. The claim that 𝓜_M(P) differs between regions follows from the declared capabilities of M at each region.

*Constraint:* the language "not directly observable" is inadmissible as a universal classification for any declared phenomenon. The correct statement is always scale-conditional: a phenomenon that is mechanism-unresolved at the Primary Region may be locus-resolved at larger scales through the same M applied at that scale. The observable record confirms this for I-T, I-S, and I-E.

**Full-operator admissibility condition.** A → B → R is admissible when M establishes: (1) observable values at declared loci, (2) independently grounded directed relations between those loci, and (3) at least one directed relational continuation in which the target of one declared relation is the source of an immediately succeeding declared relation — that is, at least one interior locus t such that t = target(e) = source(f) for independently declared edges e and f, each with provenance through M.

The minimal admissible interior is: s → t → u, where s, t, u are independently declared loci and both edges have independent observable provenance through M. The interior locus t is the condition: t is no longer merely a boundary locus — it has an observable incoming relation and an observable continuing relation. B has something to accumulate. A operates on the directed distinctions; B accumulates only the declared immediate continuation; R evaluates the resulting locally organized directed field.

Where that interior cannot be established through M — where M can declare the relation but cannot independently resolve its constituent loci — the primary kernel Δ → Σ is the correct kernel. It acts on what M can actually declare.

*This condition replaces the retired ρ_P transition criterion.* OC-ρP-1 (transition threshold not derived as computable criterion) is closed: the threshold is not numerical. It is structural — the presence or absence of a mechanism-resolvable interior locus in the declared relational structure. OC-ρP-2 (declaration-imposed ceiling) is dissolved: it was a consequence of the matrix-rank representation, which has been replaced by the mechanism-resolution condition. No component ceiling exists under the full-operator admissibility condition.

*Constraint:* no continuation, predecessor, successor, boundary closure, or operator result may be supplied by the mathematics itself. Every element of the admissible interior must be traceable to an observable through M.

**Primary Region invariants — retired quantities.** The previous Primary Region invariants entry declared: rank(Im Δ), rank(Im Σ), ρ_P. These are retired (Origin declaration, 2026-09-23). The Origin question of what observable relational condition rank(Im Δ) was intended to distinguish is answered: relational distinguishability of the Δ output (see I-S below) is the condition, read from the Δ output without matrix construction. rank(Im Δ), rank(Im Σ), and ρ_P were representation layers over that condition and are not reinstated. The full-operator admissibility condition above is the kernel boundary criterion. The implementation constraint carries forward: a nonzero-entry count is not a rank and is not a substitute for any retired quantity.

**Classical and quantum as resolving-power conditions on M.** The framework derives a unification result from the observational resolution principle and the full-operator admissibility condition.

What has been called "classical physics" is the correct description of the declared relational structure when 𝓜_M(P) = 0 — when M can declare the phenomenon but cannot resolve its internal mechanism. The primary kernel Δ → Σ produces the primary-kernel prediction: directed distinctions across declared relations, with local antisymmetric coupling, but without B accumulation along a mechanism-resolvable interior. Where the mechanism is not resolvable by M, the primary kernel is both correct and complete — it describes everything M can declare.

What has been called "quantum physics" names the phenomena that appear at scales where 𝓜_M(P) = 0: the observed outcomes of relational processes whose internal mechanism M cannot yet resolve. These are not violations of primary-kernel predictions. They are the correct relational output observed at a scale where M cannot instantiate the mechanism. The discrepancy between primary-kernel output and the observed result is not a mystery requiring a separate formalism. It is the signature of 𝓜_M(P) = 0: the phenomenon is in the range of M, but its mechanism is not yet resolvable at this scale.

At larger scales where 𝓜_M(P) = 1, the same relational structure is locus-resolved. The mechanism is directly in the range of M. The full operator chain A → B → R is admissible. What was mechanism-unresolved at one scale is locus-resolved at the scale where the mechanism becomes declarable through M — not because the relational structure changed, but because M can now declare the interior.

*What has been called classical and what has been called quantum are not two regimes of nature. They are two resolving-power conditions on M applied to the same declared relational structure.*

There are no transitions that are forbidden by the declared relational structure. There are transitions whose internal relational mechanism M cannot resolve at the available scale. The primary kernel, which correctly describes what M can declare at that scale, predicts zero for these transitions. The observed nonzero result is not a violation. It is the relational structure being operative at a scale where M cannot instantiate its mechanism. The "classically forbidden" designation has provenance not observed or incomplete: it attributes to the transition a property that belongs to M's resolving power at the scale of observation.

*Support:* Derived from the observational resolution principle (𝓜_M) and the full-operator admissibility condition. Framework-level finding — not yet independently derived from the operator fixed-point. OC-CQ-1 declared: formal derivation of the boundary between mechanism-unresolved and locus-resolved regimes as a function of 𝓜_M from the operator declarations alone, without appeal to established physics frameworks.

**Primary Region phenomena — derived invariants (I-T, I-S, I-E, I-RE).** Four invariants derived from the Primary Region operator structure are stated in `derived_invariants.rs` (Kernel V8) and grounded in `validation_record.md`. Their observational status is scale-conditional per the observational resolution principle above. The language "not directly observable" is replaced throughout by the scale-conditional classification.

*I-T (Tunneling):* At any declared transition edge where ε[e] > 0, the directed inertia contrast Δ(ι)[e] is nonzero — that is, at every declared transition edge where a relational energy contrast is established through M, the contrast is present. Consequence: ε[e] has a nonzero floor at every such edge; τ[v] is bounded above. The formal derivation of why ε[e] > 0 follows from the operator structure rather than from a separately imposed boundary condition is open — OC-T-1. *Observational status:* expression-observed at the Primary Region (ℛ_M = 1, 𝓜_M = 0) — nonzero ε[e] is in the observable record at declared transition edges where the primary kernel alone, acting on unresolved mechanism, would predict zero contrast; the mechanism producing the nonzero contrast is not yet resolvable by M at this scale. Locus-resolved at larger scales (ℛ_M = 1, 𝓜_M = 1) — transition rates at declared edges are directly in the range of M, with both the source locus ε and the declared continuation independently declarable through M. The claim that absence of this condition precludes energy transfer is a framework-level necessity statement — open condition OC-T-3.

*I-S (Superposition):* The declared relational condition in which multiple directed distinctions are simultaneously operative across declared edges before B accumulation resolves them (Origin declaration, 2026-09-23). *Relational distinguishability:* the Δ output field carries relational distinguishability when there exist declared edges e and f such that Δ(x)[e] and Δ(x)[f] are not proportional — there is no scalar k with Δ(x)[e][c] = k · Δ(x)[f][c] for every component c. The condition is read from the Δ output computed over declared projections through M; it is a calculated result, compared with the observable record through M. No matrix construction, no decomposition, no threshold. *Resolution:* the superposition condition resolves structurally when M establishes at least one interior locus (full-operator admissibility condition above), not at a numerical threshold. *Implementation hold:* `are_proportional` in `derived_invariants.rs` (Kernel V8) is held — its result depends on edge order when a Δ vector is 0.0 in every component, and its tolerance is used in two units. No code change is authorized. *Observational status:* expression-observed at the Primary Region (ℛ_M = 1, 𝓜_M = 0) — distributed directed contrast across multiple declared edges is in the observable record through M; the mechanism by which multiple directed contrasts are simultaneously operative is not yet resolvable by M at this scale. Locus-resolved at larger scales (ℛ_M = 1, 𝓜_M = 1) — the same distributed directed contrast is in the range of M with constituent loci independently declarable through M. *Previous formulation retired:* rank(Im Δ) > 1 was the prior mathematical expression of this condition; relational distinguishability replaces it. The observational record stands. OC-S-1 is closed: the kernel boundary is the full-operator admissibility condition, not a numerical threshold. OC-S-2 (restated): confirm relational distinguishability of the Δ output in declared systems beyond the Primary Region. OC-S-3 (restated): formally derive that the absence of relational distinguishability throughout a declared history precludes self-organization within D.

*I-E (Entanglement):* A declared edge e=(s,t) with 𝟙[e]=1 establishes a relational constraint between s and t that is preserved under the operators regardless of spatial separation within D. Spatial separation is a C projection — the declared edge relation is prior to it. When 𝟙[e]=0 and ε[e]=0, the relational constraint is absent — this is the admissible statement of its removal within D. *Observational status:* expression-observed at the Primary Region (ℛ_M = 1, 𝓜_M = 0) — the relational constraint is operative and its expression is in the observable record through M; the internal mechanism constituting the edge relation is not yet resolvable by M at this scale. Locus-resolved at larger scales (ℛ_M = 1, 𝓜_M = 1) — relational constraints between independently declared loci are directly in the range of M, with both s and t independently declarable through M. The claim that 𝟙[e]=0 everywhere precludes collective organization is a framework-level necessity statement — open condition OC-E-3.

*I-RE (Relational Evolution):* Relational evolution rate is monotonically decreasing from the Primary Region outward. τ[v] at the Primary Region produces the smallest declared relational progression intervals in D. The Φ[v] 78-decade span confirmed in VR-Φ-01 is the observable expression of this invariant. Larger-scale persistent structure is Primary Region relational evolution accumulated through B activation. The claim that all larger-scale structure is formally downstream of Primary Region evolution is a framework-level necessity statement — open condition OC-RE-1.

*Joint necessity (framework-level observation):* I-T, I-S, and I-E are jointly necessary conditions for any self-organizing, evolving, observable system to exist within D. More precisely: the conditions that make observable data possible — the constitution of directed relations, the capacity for directed distinction, the existence of relational continuation — are mechanism-level structure. They are operative in every observation. They cannot themselves be mechanism-resolved through M, because M requires them to operate. At the Primary Region, where 𝓜_M = 0, they are expression-observed through their relational outputs in the range of M. At larger scales, where 𝓜_M = 1, they are locus-resolved — the constituent loci and relations are independently declarable. In neither case are they absent from the observable record. What changes is M's resolving power. The I-S component of joint necessity is declared in terms of relational distinguishability (2026-09-23). See `derived_invariants.rs` (Kernel V8) joint necessity preamble.

*The notation says what the operators are; the constraints say what may not be added. The admissibility condition says what every quantity must be traceable to. The relational direction condition says that every declared relation has exactly one admissible direction. Bounded over D. No claim beyond D.*

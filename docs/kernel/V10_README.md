## The relational declaration

Every V10 item furthers these three, and none may contradict them:

1. **Relation is the primitive** (Df1). The minimal relation registered already includes the observer's participation (Df4).
2. **Relational evolution is one-directional and continuous** (Df2, O1). It never runs backward and never stops, including while we analyse it. Looking back is analysis, not reverse evolution (Df2a).
3. **Energy, connection and freedom are observable throughout relational evolution** (G1, G4). The kernel expresses them as tunneling, entanglement and superposition. What we measure is their degree at declared resolution, and what each means in a given system is declared for its domain (R9a). An intervention or a coarse resolution bounds their degree, never their presence.

**[Origin restates the three as the foundations (2026-11-23, 59th):** *"These are the foundations of what we are building: number one relation is primitive; numeral 2 energy, connection, and freedom are observable in all data these are also described as tunneling, entanglement, and superposition; numeral 3 the direction of relational evolution is mono directional and never stops its pace is dictated by the scale at which the analysis is directed."* Items 1 to 3 above stand, and Origin's order is 1 (relation), 2 (energy, connection, freedom), 3 (direction). This restatement adds two things:
- **(2) "in all data".** Every registration is to give all three, however it was registered. In the kernel, a registration of the relations gives energy, connection and freedom together, with no locus value constructed (`RelationalField::from_relations`, 59th). Where a grade still cannot be formed, it is named as a gap, never filled.
- **(3) "its pace is dictated by the scale at which the analysis is directed".** The step of relational evolution is the step between consecutive intersections at the declared scale of analysis (δ, the declared relations, the ordering T6). Pace is read in those steps, never in time (W15), so a finer scale reads more steps of the same evolution. *Generator's reading, for Origin to confirm.* No form changes: every change form already reads one step of the declared sequence (Df3).**]**

## The structure in one line

```
 … --> { --> --> --> --> --> } --> …
       └─ cycle of analysis ─┘
```

*(Origin, 2026-11-16: `{-->-->-->-->-->}`, "this is what we're talking about".)*

- **The arrows are relational evolution.** Every arrow points the same way. None points back, and none may be read or computed as pointing back (Df2).
- **The braces are the scope of analysis.** The observer chooses them (R10). They are not a boundary of the field, which runs on past both braces and keeps evolving while we analyse (Df3).
- **Inside the braces, reading is free.** We may read in either direction, from the last arrow, or back to an arrow's predecessor. No reading turns an arrow around (Df2a).
- **Each arrow is read with its immediate neighbours, and every arrow in one pass** (Df3: encompass, do not blur).
- **The next braces pair with this cycle's output** (K5). Arrows already accumulated are not accumulated again (scope). They may be read again (Df2a).
- **An arrow with no neighbour inside the braces** calls for wider braces (more registrations), never a reversed arrow.

## 1.2 The variables of the ground: energy, connection, freedom, circulation

These are the variables every analysis reads. Each is present by ground (G1, G2, G4). What is measured is its **degree at δ**.

**Three distinct variables, one relational object (Origin, 2026-11-21).** Energy, freedom and connection are distinct; none reduces to another. They are read from the same declared relations, through one structural object, the minor pattern P(F) (§2.4, V8), and are related as follows:

- **per minor at δ, freedom and connection are complementary**: a minor is resolved (independent variation) or not resolved (shared structure);
  - *Amended (Origin, 2026-11-21, 7th):* **structure is persistence; change is freedom.** Every reading is complementary in this sense at δ: held by a constraint (structure) or permitted to change (freedom). Freedom is read from change (§2.5, PC1–PC3); connection is shared structure, in state or in change. The full chain is §2.0;
- **energy moves minors toward or across zero**; a crossing is where energy exchange becomes visible in the structure. Energy also has its own carrier and flows whether or not anything reorganizes;
- **all three are held over relational evolution.**

**Readings are formed over a declared sequence (Origin, 2026-11-21; Df6).** A frozen frame is not a relational system (O1, Df3), and all operators and variables occur together (Df6). Every ground-variable reading is formed over a declared sequence of at least two consecutive registrations of the same declared relations, ordered by an observable property of the process (T6). Over the sequence each minor does one of three things:

| Over the sequence, m_ij(e,f)… | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| stays not resolved | **connection**: shared structure persisting |
| stays resolved with one sign | **freedom**: independent variation persisting |
| does neither (it crosses, or changes state) | **reorganization**: the structure changes |

*Amended (Origin, 2026-11-21, 7th).* Under structure = persistence, the table reads: **stays not resolved** → structure, shared (connection, proportional grade); **stays resolved with one sign** → structure, a persistent distinct arrangement (not freedom); **does neither** → change of arrangement: freedom (PC3). Freedom is read from change at every level (§2.5). The first reading is kept above for the record; its code (`freedom`) is kept as the declared comparison `v2_held_arrangement`.

A single registration is a declaration finding (*domain not fully declared: no process sequence*). The operators A, B, R are still evaluated per registration as building blocks; this amends what counts as a ground-variable reading, not the operators.

**Observable fact and calculated variable (Origin, 2026-11-21).** The *observable fact* is that energy, connection and freedom are present in every registration; absence is not an available observation, and no calculation tests it. The *calculated variable* is the degree the operators yield, over a declared topology and sequence at declared δ. When a reading cannot be calculated, the result is a finding about the declaration, never about the system:

- **Primary Region**: no interior locus is declared (T7). **Only the sustained grade of connection needs an interior.** Energy, freedom, and the identity and proportional grades of connection are readable at every region, Primary included, wherever two components and a sequence are declared; or
- **Domain not fully declared**: the domain declaration (R9a) lacks a carrier, a component, a relation, a registration, a process sequence or a finer δ.

Either way the analysis returns to the declaration (G4a, W5): finer δ or a wider record boundary, never a reversed edge (W8) and never a substitute value. A calculated degree of zero at δ is returned unchanged and read under W2. Implemented in `src/ground_variables.rs` (`DeclarationGap`, `FindingKind`, `GroundReading::findings`); the mathematics is in Part 2 (V1–V3, V8). The V9 readers in `src/derived_invariants.rs` are unchanged.

### Method

- Declare before running; commit the declaration first; disclose what is already known.
- Node-derived fields make closure an identity; only independently registered edges test closure.
- Registrations are not mappings. Radii, adjusted constants and composition edges are Projected. Never compare against an adjusted value as if it were a registration.
- Provider intervals are an interim δ (M3 corners), not Z3. Seek repeated registrations.
- Check the numerics: a central value outside its own interval means the solver, not the data.
- Kernel text names a specific system only inside a marked **Example (application)** block (boundary rule, Origin 2026-11-14).
- **Decompose, do not simplify** (Declared, Origin, 2026-11-23, 41st: *"we are decomposing complex systems but we're not simplifying them"*). The aim is to analyze how relation, as the primitive, and energy, connection and freedom work together in a complex system. A reading divides the field into parts, and the parts must account for the whole. Nothing registered is dropped along the way, whether by arithmetic, by a summary standing in for its readings, or by a choice of basis or tree. In practice:
  - every part is reported with its identity, value and bound;
  - a summary is a declared projection (K6), read beside its parts, never in place of them;
  - a part not read is reported as *not evaluated*, never filled;
  - a sum of registered entries is exact, rounded once at the end;
  - a bound is never rounded below its exact value.

  Code: `src/exact.rs`. Rounding points still to bring under this rule are listed in Part 7 (OC-NUM-1).

## 2.5 Structure and freedom as persistence and change (PC) — Declared (Origin, 2026-11-21, 7th)

**Principle (Origin, 2026-11-21, as stated).** Where relations permit change, freedom increases. Where constraints limit change, structure increases. **Structure is persistence; change is freedom.** Connection is shared structure: relations held together by a constraint.

**Object.** Declared edges e, components c, registrations t = 0..n−1 ordered by an observable property of the process (T6), with edge field A_t[c][e] and half-width δA_t[c][e]. Change is read only between consecutive registrations (Df3), the prior always the earlier (Df2): Δ_s[c][e] = A_{s+1}[c][e] − A_s[c][e] (A_p, K5), δΔ_s = δA_s + δA_{s+1}. ### I-SC-D15 in full (Proposed, Generator, 2026-11-23, 37th)

**Object.** A scale multiplies each entry A[c][e] by a factor λ ≠ 0. It scales the value and its repeatability bound together (δ is declared in the registered unit): A′ = λ·A, δA′ = |λ|·δA. The factor has one of four patterns:

| Kind | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| **C** | one per component, λ_c |
| **E** | one per edge, λ_e |
| **CE** | a component factor times an edge factor, λ_c·λ_e |
| **X** | one per entry, λ_ce |

Each kind is either the same at every registration (**over the run**) or positive and possibly different between registrations (**per registration**).

**(i) One rule.** Every V10 form at δ is a sum of terms, each a product of entries. Its half-width is built from the same products: δ sums for first-order forms, the product bound for minors. If a scale gives every term the same factor κ, the form and its half-width scale by κ and |κ|, so the classification is unchanged. If two terms get different factors, some field changes: take the terms equal, so the form is 0 at zero repeatability, and the scale moves it off 0. *Amended after the Verifier's review (2026-11-23, 39th), before declaration:* the rule is stated for **the forms listed in (ii)**, under the scale model above (value and repeatability bound scaled together), and claims nothing about other forms. In a passing cell, what is preserved is every **classification**: resolved or not at δ, and held, reorganizing or crossing over the sequence. **Signed outputs transform as specified in (iv)**: first-order values by λ, minors by λ_ie·λ_jf. The values are not preserved. In a failing cell, two mechanisms occur. Different factors on two terms can break an exact cancellation. A factor that differs between registrations can turn an unchanged entry into a non-zero change.

**(ii) The table** (✓ unchanged on every field; ✗ some field changes):

| Form | Terms | C | E | CE | X | Per registration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PC1 | one entry at s and s+1 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ every kind |
| PC2(b) | Δ_s[e]·Δ_{s+1}[f], one component | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ every kind |
| PC2(a); V1 coupled change | Δ_i[e]·Δ_j[f], Δ_j[e]·Δ_i[f] | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ every kind |
| PC3; V1 proportional | m_ij(e, f) on A | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ C, E, CE |
| V1 identity | A[c][e] − A[c][f] | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ C |
| V1 sustained (Ξ(B(A))) | entries of one component within two steps of e | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ C |
| K7 closure, in state | Σ s_e·A[c][e] | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ C |
| K7 closure, on change (I-SC-D10) | Σ s_e·Δ[c][e] | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ every kind |
| Energy floor ε > δ_ε (V3) | \|A(carrier)[e]\| | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ every kind |

For a single minor the exact condition is λ_ie·λ_jf = λ_je·λ_if, which every CE scale meets. For identity it is λ_ce = λ_cf. For a sustained-grade or closure reading, one factor across the edges it sums is sufficient.

**(iii) Over the run and per registration.** State forms read each registration on its own: PC3's two states, the identity, proportional and sustained grades, and the state closure. So a positive factor of a passing kind may differ between registrations. Change forms compare two registrations, Δ′ = λ_{s+1}·A_{s+1} − λ_s·A_s, so the factor must be the same at both. This is why Z1a's run-constant parts must be declared the same over the run.

**(iv) Sign.** A negative factor that is the same at every registration is a declared mapping (W14). Every cell holds as stated with the signed factors; for PC2(a) and PC3 the condition λ_ie·λ_jf = λ_je·λ_if applies to the signed factors. Every resolution and every held state is unchanged. First-order values (A, Δ, Ξ, closures) change sign with λ, and a minor with λ_ie·λ_jf. A sign that differs between registrations is a reversal of sense mid-run. It is outside this statement, and it can produce PC3 crossings. This restates PC-D3 and the gain-sign condition (Z1a) for every kind.

**(v) Node-derived fields** admit one scale on their relations: C, shared by every locus of a component. A factor per locus is not a scale of A, since λ_s·x_s − λ_t·x_t ≠ λ·(x_s − x_t). It is Z1a's unshared gain, (iii) of the correction, which enters the state and change bounds. The kernel reads the identity, proportional and sustained grades on node-derived fields only, so their E and X cells cannot arise in code. They are shown by the arithmetic in (i).

**(vi) What follows for declared gains (Z1a).** A gain is a positive factor fixed over the run. A gain of a kind that a form's cell passes changes none of that form's classifications and enters only reported magnitudes. A gain of any other kind must enter that form's bound. ### I-SC-D16 in full (Proposed, Generator, 2026-11-23, 38th)

**Object.** An edge-registered series, a stated spanning tree (one per connected part) and its fundamental cycles C_1 … C_r, one per chord (K7, `cycle_basis`). A closed walk w has signed edge counts c_w(e) (+1 along, −1 against, summed over its occurrences). Its closure on an edge field g is Σ_e c_w(e)·g[e]. Its bound is the sum of the term bounds over its occurrences: δA in state, δΔ on change, each widened by the gains that differ between edges (I-SC-D15 (vi)).

**(i) Exact decomposition.** Let n_i = c_w(chord_i). Then c_w − Σ n_i·c_{C_i} is a cycle vector on tree edges alone, and a tree carries none, so c_w = Σ n_i·c_{C_i}. Hence, for every edge field:
- in state: closure_w(A_t) = Σ n_i·closure_{C_i}(A_t);
- on change: closure_w(Δ_s) = Σ n_i·closure_{C_i}(Δ_s) = closure_w(A_{s+1}) − closure_w(A_s) (I-SC-D10).

So |closure_w| ≤ Σ |n_i|·|closure_{C_i}|. If every basis closure holds, |closure_w| ≤ Σ |n_i|·h_{C_i}. `express_in_basis` already gave this for the state value; it now holds and is tested on change too.

**(ii) At δ the basis does not settle every cycle.** Fundamental cycles share tree edges. A walk that combines them can cancel a shared edge's value but not its bound in the sum Σ |n_i|·h_{C_i}. Its own bound counts that edge only where the walk uses it. Worked example, the diamond 0→1→2, 0→2, 0→3→2:
- every bound is 1/8, with e0 = 3/8, e4 = −3/8 and the rest 0;
- with tree {e2, e0, e3}, both basis closures hold at their bound (3/8 and −3/8 against 3/8);
- the outer cycle 0→1→2←3←0 reads 6/8 against its own 4/8, so it is resolved;
- with tree {e0, e1, e3}, one basis closure is resolved (−6/8 against 4/8).

So the number of resolved basis closures depends on the stated tree. "Every basis closure holds" is a reading of that basis, reported with its tree. It does not mean every cycle closes. At δ = 0 the basis does settle every cycle: all basis closures 0 ⇔ every closure 0.

**(iii) Every cycle holds ⇔ within δ of a node difference.** Every simple cycle holds at δ exactly when there is a node field x with |g[e] − (x[s] − x[t])| ≤ b[e] for every relation. Here b[e] is the term bound the closures use. Proof: these are difference constraints. They are feasible exactly when no cycle of the constraint graph has negative weight, and a cycle traversing w has weight Σ b − closure_w or Σ b + closure_w. A closed walk decomposes into simple cycles whose bounds add, so simple cycles suffice. The same holds on change, with Δ and δΔ.

This is the converse of PC-D4. A node-derived field closes every cycle by identity, and an edge-registered field that holds every closure at δ is consistent at δ with one. **A resolved closure is a reading that no node-derived field could give.** That reading is the content of ER-1. The node field x is a mathematical witness in the proof and in the test. It is never constructed or reported by a reading (§2.0). *Scope (the Verifier's clarification, 39th):* the equivalence is for the **per-edge interval bounds of one state, or of one change**. A node field that fits one registration and another that fits the next say nothing about one calibration fitting both. (iii) gives no joint witness across registrations: a run-constant offset or gain is read through Z1a and D10, not through this equivalence. **[Under W12 as amended (42nd), pending Origin:** the "⇐" direction of (iii), that some node field fits whenever every cycle holds, rests on a legacy result, the theory of difference constraints and negative cycles. It has not been identified as necessary for analysis. The "⇒" direction is PC-D4 and stays. Recommended: the "⇐" direction is fenced as a **declared intersection (C)**, citing that theory, and is not a kernel result. The test's Bellman–Ford witness (`node_field_within`) then checks the cited intersection only.**] **[Decided by Origin (44th): fenced. The "⇐" direction of (iii) is a declared intersection (C), citing the theory of difference constraints and negative cycles. It is not a kernel result. The "⇒" direction is PC-D4.]**

**(iv) A failing chord does not fail every walk through it.** closure_w = n_i·closure_{C_i} + Σ_{j≠i} n_j·closure_{C_j}, and the other terms can cancel it. It is sufficient, not necessary, for w to be resolved that |n_i·closure_{C_i}| − Σ_{j≠i} |n_j|·|closure_{C_j}| exceeds w's own bound. In the diamond with tree {e0, e1, e3}, the triangle 0→3→2→0 passes through chord e4, whose basis closure is resolved, and holds: −3/8 against 3/8.

**What it implies for readings.**
- A closure reading over a basis states its tree.
- "Every cycle closes at δ" needs every simple cycle, or the equivalent difference-constraint test.
- A resolved basis closure is itself a resolved cycle, whatever the tree.

The kernel has no all-cycles test yet. One could check (iii) in polynomial time by shortest paths, which is engineering for Origin to decide.

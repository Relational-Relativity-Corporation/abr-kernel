# Part 1 — Origin's Declaration

## What this document is committed to

A variable set is not a model. It is not a theoretical framework.
It is a declaration of what M can map into D at a given region —
stated without importing the theoretical structures that classical
or quantum physics built on top of the observable record.

No variable in this document is a primitive by assumption. Every
variable is declared as an observable quantity — something an
instrument reported, or something directly computable from declared
measurement outputs through a formula that is part of M. Where a
quantity requires theoretical mediation to reach the instrument,
that mediation must be declared explicitly as part of M before the
quantity enters D.

This document answers a question the operator documents deliberately
leave open:

**What is admissibly available to the operators before any
topology, coupling, or kernel is declared?**

## Methodological context — how the variable inventory was built

The variable sets in this document were derived initially by treating
the Standard Model and related legacy frameworks as an observational
inventory — a record of quantities that the physics community has
found necessary to account for the observable record across declared
regions. This is the correct use of legacy frameworks: a starting
point for the inventory, not a source of admissibility.

Most theoretical frameworks in physics were constructed before direct
observation of the phenomena they address was possible. Atomic
structure, nuclear structure, and particle interactions were theorized
under conditions of indirect inference — from scattering experiments,
spectral lines, decay products, and statistical distributions. The
mathematics built under those conditions was often extraordinarily
successful at prediction. But the quantities it named were not always
derived from direct observables. Many were derived from what made the
math work under conditions of indirect inference, not from direct
measurement.

The consequence for this document: a quantity appears in a regional
variable set because it passed an independent admissibility test —
not because a legacy framework endorses it. The test is always: what
is the direct measurement that puts this quantity into D through M,
independently of any theoretical commitment?

The inertial response observable — what the measurement record has
historically named mass — passed this test. The framework admits it
as relational inertia ι[v]: the inertial response of a declared locus
under the declared non-acceleration condition through M. Rest mass
is inadmissible (SF-PR-16) — it requires a rest frame not observable
through M. The observable that passed the test is ι[v], not rest mass.
Within the declared framework, the observable admitted through M is
represented by ι[v]; the primitive interpretation differs from legacy
frameworks, the numerical values from the measurement record do not.

Charge passed this test. Both ι[v] and q[v] are directly traceable
through M at every declared region independently of any framework
that also uses them.

Z_eff passes this test at the Atomic Region. Ionization energy is a
direct measurement. Z_eff is extractable from that measurement through
a declared mapping. Its admissibility rests on the direct measurement,
not on atomic theory as a framework.

Quantities that did not pass this test are absent from the variable
sets regardless of their role in legacy frameworks. Their absence is
not a claim that they are wrong. It is a statement that they are not
directly traceable to an observable through M at the declared region
under the admissibility conditions of this framework.

## Support classification

Every finding in this document carries one of three declared
support levels. These are not a hierarchy of authority —
observation is not subordinate to derivation. They are
distinctions in kind.

**Observed:** directly supported by M. The quantity was
reported by an instrument, or computed from instrument
outputs through a formula that is part of M. No framework
inference required. Example: Φ[v] values computed from
measured ι[v] and τ[v].

**Inferred:** follows from observed data under the declared
framework. The connection to M is through the framework's
declared architecture, not through a direct instrument
reading. Example: region-specific thresholds extracted from
the observed Φ distribution; bound neutron stability
attributed to coherence propagation.

**Derived:** proven from the operator mathematics. The
connection to M is through the formal operator expressions
and their declared constraints. Example: 𝟙[e] = 1 iff
R(A(x))[e]_antisymmetric ≠ 0 — proven from R operator.

Where a finding carries no label, it is observed unless
otherwise stated. Where a finding is inferred or derived,
it is labeled explicitly. Verifier scrutiny is highest
at the boundary between inferred and derived — where a
framework inference awaits formal derivation.

The distinction between observation and framework interpretation
is maintained throughout this document. Where a measured value
is followed by a framework reading of that value, the two are
explicitly separated.

## Per-Region Variable Sets

The three-layer template applies at every region. Layer 1
(declaration protocol) is identical across all regions.
Layer 2 (primitive variables) is declared here per region.
Layer 3 (operator output) applies identical operators at
every region — what changes is what they act on.

A primitive is not simply what cannot be derived from the
operators. A primitive is what M must declare at a given
region because the information it carries was lost in the
act of bounding the locus at that scale. Every time M draws
a boundary around a compound system and calls it a single
locus, some interior relational structure is collapsed. If
that collapsed structure has observable consequences at the
declared scale that the operators cannot recover from ι[v]
and q[v] alone, a new primitive is required to carry that
information.

A quantity is a genuine primitive at region R if and only if:
1. It is directly traceable to an observable through M at R
2. It carries information about sub-locus relational structure
   that was collapsed when M bounded the locus at region R
3. That information has observable consequences at region R
   that the operators cannot produce from ι[v] and q[v] alone

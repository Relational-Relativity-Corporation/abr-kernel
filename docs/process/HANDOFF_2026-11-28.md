### 4.1 Connection and freedom are not yet calculated as variables in text — **in progress**

The Generator proposed, at each step x→y on the field so far:

- **connection**: the subject held at x, the stimulated synapses on the path; continuity onward from y as a possible second part;
- **freedom**: the stimulated branches at x.

It noted that energy's carrier would then be connection's sign, so the two would overlap.

Origin: *"My sense of it is that depending upon the relationship between energy and connectivity there is more or less freedom"*.

**The Generator's restatement, awaiting Origin's answer:**

- **connection** at x: +1 if any synapse leaving x has been stimulated, −1 if none;
- **energy**: as in the 117th;
- **freedom**: connection less energy, step by step, accumulated (linear, no ratio):

| Connection | Energy | Freedom | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| +1 | +1 | 0 | energy follows connection |
| +1 | −1 | +2 | energy breaks from connection; freedom exercised |
| −1 | −1 | 0 | new ground |
| −1 | +1 | — | cannot occur |

**The question put to Origin:** is this freedom *exercised* (above), or freedom *available* (the stimulated branches at x set against the movement energy brings, decomposed spoke by spoke)? The Generator recommended exercised first. Then: record as the 118th and start the tests.

### 4.2 The operators do the least

On text's graphs, B and Ξ reduce exactly to A and the readback halves, and read mostly how busy an element is (Ξ is dominated by −n·out(x)). Energy's ±1 is a sign decided at reading, which the operators accumulate. The linear algebra carries the field, but the readings that matter are made beside it.

### 4.3 The order is kept beside the operators, not read through them

This is the previous handoff's §4 gap 1, still open. The comparisons that keep the order (the degrees, alongside, `Degree.parts`) are legacy, admitted by Origin (108th).

## 7. The lessons to carry forward

- **Three of the Generator's departures were caught today, each by Origin's question:**
  - the split, legacy mathematics presented as an observation;
  - comparing neurons when Origin had said synapses;
  - computing parts apart ("independent synapses") when Origin had said one field.

  Each time the Generator moved to build before the mapping was settled. **Settle the mapping in Origin's words before code, every time**, even when asked to "write this".
- **Check a proposal's reduction before proposing it.** On complete radial graphs the split's potential part is only the ends divided by 2n, which a numerical check showed in a minute. Check what a construct reduces to on text's actual shape first.
- **A test can pass on a wrong claim.** The first draft of the whole-field-change test claimed every synapse's passage changes at every step; it doesn't (u = y, v ≠ x gives 0). Write the claim from the formula, not from the picture.
- Earlier traps still hold:
  - byte offsets in text containing ⇧ or ’ (find by character: `after_chars`);
  - forming the full field on long text (use `RadialField`);
  - reading a field at an element it never declared (the example's fault in `v10_subject`).
- "Coherence" already names §2.10 (73rd) in `src/coherence.rs`. The 116th's structure is named `Activity` so the two aren't confused.

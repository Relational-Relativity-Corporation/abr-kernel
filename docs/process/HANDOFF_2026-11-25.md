## 3. The state of the work, honestly

**What is relational science here, and what is not.** Everything that generated text (`route.rs`, `subject.rs`, `connect.rs`, `words.rs`, the binaries `route`, `split`, `explore`, `steer`, `words`, `both`, `energy`, `ideas`) is **application code formulated in V10's terms but not computed by the kernel**. Its results are real measurements of that code, and they are useful evidence of what a coherent stream needs. They are not Kernel V10 relational analysis. The kernel does run in `kfield.rs`/`kread` (beside the choice, over route elements, not the alphabet) and in `afield.rs`/`tests/kernel_use.rs`.

**What is done right, and verified:** `abr-relational-ai/text-anticipation/src/afield.rs` declares the **character field** as Origin directed:
- every character a locus, nothing cut;
- every ordered pair a relation, with identity and basis, feedback on cycles;
- identity spokes beside the field;
- components kept apart: input, produced, declared witnesses;
- exact counts, checked before conversion.

`tests/kernel_use.rs` (12 tests) confirms that relation is the primitive; that A, B and Ξ are linear and composed (B from A, Ξ from B); and that freedom, connection and energy are calculated as the kernel defines them, all together (Df6), with provenance and one direction. **Every test has been shown to fail** under a deliberate break, in the application and in the kernel itself. `KernelReport` can only be made from a kernel field.

**What the measurements showed** (application code, three books):
- **Counting** recalls and splices what it has read. It never reproduces text it has not read.
- **The 30-character bound and the joins** give a stream of real words (93–99.5%) that recombines the input without copying.
- **Energy balance** at the character scale is exact under any chooser.
- **Coherence needs two scales.** Phrase continuity comes from routes held. Subject continuity comes from content off the field's general direction. No chooser built so far achieves both at the level of the books.

## 4. The lesson to carry forward

The recurring failure of this session (the 84th and the 90th): **reaching for familiar text-processing methods and describing them in V10's vocabulary**; working around the kernel when it was slow instead of fixing it; patching symptoms (loops, copying, cut words) instead of deriving from the field; and continuing after the problem had been named. The rules in `CLAUDE.md` now say:
- **no output that the kernel did not produce;**
- where the kernel cannot produce a reading, the result is a **named gap**, not a stand-in;
- **tests first**, each shown to fail;
- settle the mapping with Origin before writing code;
- label any result not produced by the kernel in its first line.

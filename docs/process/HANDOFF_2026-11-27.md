## 4. How we are decomposing the system — what the Generator observes

Origin: *"I feel as though we're missing something"*. The 41st: *decompose, do not simplify — nothing registered is dropped by arithmetic, by a summary standing in for its parts*. Measured against it, the text work has four gaps. They are offered for Origin, not decided.

1. **The operators act on a summary.** Each registration is the radial graphs *as they stand*: A = the sum of every step so far. B, Ξ and the readback act on that sum. The order is kept beside it (the steps), but **the operators never act on the decomposition, only on the total**. That is why every reading at the current point lost the order and fell into repetition or into the busiest junction (the space), and why B and Ξ reduce to how busy an element is. The parts are the steps, each at its own point in evolution; the readings compose the sum from them but are not read *through* them.
2. **The variables are named on the text field but not decomposed in it.** The readings are A, B, Ξ, the step and the indications. Freedom (the branching), connection (the continuity) and energy (what moves the system) have been *assigned* to readings in discussion ("alongside looks like connection"), never formed as the three parts of one decomposition of the field. Energy in text is still the step itself (the trial carrier), the same for every way forward, so it can decompose nothing.
3. **The field's own decomposition is unused.** Every field of relations holds a potential part and a balanced part (the 99th, the 100th's orthogonal split). Text's field is one route: its potential part is a single unit from where the text began to where it is (energy content, which cancels everywhere else); **everything else is balanced, made of turns** — each return to an element is one revolution of the helix (107th), and the degrees of freedom are exactly those turns (each earlier point at the current element to now). The text's structure lives in its turns, and the turns have not been read as parts: they are compared only by literal identity of their arrivals.
4. **The scales are separate fields, not one field decomposed.** The word scale is a second radial field over word elements. The one link tested is that a word step's character route keeps only its ends in the readback's balance (the 97th: internal relations cancel). A word is a region of the character field; a sentence a region of words. One field read at several scales, its parts at each scale related to the parts at the next, has not been built.

**The Generator's reading of what is missing:** relation across a difference — what transformers do with learned similarity — may be reachable here *without* a similarity measure if the turns are taken as the parts: two turns are related through the relations they share (their turn sums of the same field, their readbacks at matching elements), not through literal agreement of their last few steps. That would be the field decomposed into its turns, read by the operators, at every scale. **This is a direction to examine with Origin, not a design.** The rule holds: no familiar method in V10's vocabulary.

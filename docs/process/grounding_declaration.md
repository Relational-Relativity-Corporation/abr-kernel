## 1. Governing ground

Within the human reference frame, relation is primitive. Observation is participation in relations already in progress.

Energy, connection and freedom are present in those relations. Antisymmetric circulation is an observational constant: a continuously present feature of relational observation in this reference frame.

*Adoption note.* The ground of relational science (`operators_notation_and_constraint.md`, PROPOSED 2026-11-12) names three observational constants: energy, connection and freedom. Adopting this declaration adds antisymmetric circulation as a fourth. It thereby adopts into the kernel the circulation constant first stated in the relational system declaration of 2026-11-13 (RS, abr-relational-chemistry, DRAFT).

These conditions govern the description. Missing measurements, incomplete representations or unresolved declarations do not establish the absence of these conditions, or the cessation of participation.

*Reconciliation with `kernel_reference.md` §11.4.* R_anti = 0 and R_anti ≠ 0 remain admissible mathematical outcomes. A calculated zero antisymmetric term is a statement about the declared relations at the declared resolution δ. It is not an absence of circulation (ground, item 9).

## 2. Observable-to-operator correspondence

Every mathematical representation states how its quantities correspond to registered observable information in the human reference frame.

The path is traceable in both directions:

registered information ↔ declared measurement and representation ↔ relational topology ↔ operators ↔ resulting response

Tracing backward means recovering provenance and identifying transformations and information loss. It does not imply that every transformation is mathematically invertible.

Each transition identifies its source information, mathematical definition, assumptions and scope, and any information introduced, combined, projected or discarded.

## 3. Required mathematical presence

The description identifies how its mathematics embodies each of the following:

- **Relation as primitive:** how the representation preserves relational participation and dependency.
- **Energy:** its observable correspondence, its mathematical reading, and its role in relational change.
- **Connection:** the topology, scopes and conditions through which relations participate.
- **Freedom:** what can vary, what constrains that variation, and how the declared mathematics expresses it.
- **Antisymmetric circulation:** its observable correspondence, mathematical definition and orientation, and its expression through the operators and the response.

Names, comments and the existence of variables do not by themselves establish these correspondences.

*Undeclared correspondence.* Where a correspondence is not declared, the description states it as *not evaluated* or *unresolved*, and names the missing declaration. That is a compliant identification. Example: on registered count fields, ι is not declared, so energy is not evaluated. It enters only as a declared comparison axis, such as laser power.

## 4. Direction and circulation

Relational evolution is monodirectional. The description explains how antisymmetric circulation is expressed consistently with that evolution.

Without an explicit derivation, antisymmetry is not equated with any of the following: reversing evolution, fabricating a reverse registration, or requiring every quantity or response to be antisymmetric.

## 5. Mathematical authority

Every formula has an attributable source, an explicit derivation, or a clearly justified domain declaration. An external mathematical method requires an explanation of its role and of its compatibility with this grounding.

Domains, bounds, units, initialization, aggregation and unavailable information are stated explicitly.

*This section does not relax `kernel_reference.md` §20.* Any quantity not declared by Origin through M before generation has provenance not observed or incomplete, whatever its source and whether or not it computes correctly. An explanation supplied after use does not meet this requirement.

## 6. Verification

Verification demonstrates these correspondences through concrete, reconstructable observations and calculations.

An adopted grounding condition is a requirement to preserve and demonstrate. Declaring it is not evidence that an implementation satisfies it. Unresolved correspondence remains explicitly unresolved.

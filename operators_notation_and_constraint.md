# The Operators — Notation and Constraint

**Metatron Dynamics, Inc.** V6. Reference data. Bounded over D. No claim beyond D.

**Admissibility.** A statement is admissible within D if and only if every quantity in it can be traced to an observable through M. Declarability is necessary but not sufficient. A quantity that is declared but not traceable to an observable through M is not inadmissible — it is not about anything in D. Declaration is never a substitute for traceability to an observable through M. Declaration is the act of stating traceability to an observable through M and taking responsibility for it. The admissibility condition is not relaxed by the act of declaration — it is what every declaration is required to satisfy.

**Domain.** D := { x ∈ ℝⁿ | n < ∞, |x[i]| < ∞ }. M : O → D, declared before anything is taken. The kernel acts on M(o) only. *Sequential observation requirement:* Phase 1 (spatial) is admissible on a single declared observation M(o). Phase 2 (persistence) requires M to declare a sequence {M(o₁), M(o₂), ..., M(oₙ)} where each oₖ is a declared observation at one relational step and the relational step is the system's own declared process step — not an externally imposed increment and not a model-generated value. A model-generated trajectory is not an observable sequence through M; it is a formal construction that may be declared within M only when the model is itself declared as a transducer from observable inputs. Cold start (E_prior = zero EdgeField) is admissible on the first declared step only. Treating cold start output as if it captures relational evolution is inadmissible — it captures the relational structure of the current observation against no prior history, not a directed difference between two observations.

**Relations.** A declared relation is either: (1) a directed spatial edge e = (s, t) where s and t are node-valued loci; or (2) a directed persistence relation connecting two complete kernel output states E_prior[e] and E_current[e] across one declared relational step, where the loci are edge-valued. *Constraint:* every relation must be traceable to an observable through M. A relation that cannot be traced to an observable through M is fabricated within D, even when it computes. There is no provenance source other than the observable. Declaration is always a declaration of traceability to an observable through M, never a substitute for it.

**Relational direction.** Every declared relation has exactly one admissible direction — the direction traceable to an observable through M. This holds without distinction for spatial relations, persistence relations, and hadronic transitions. The reverse direction requires independent observable provenance. If it does not have it, it is inadmissible. *Distinctness axiom (declared condition):* if both (s,t) and (t,s) are independently observed and declared, they are treated as distinct relations with distinct provenance — not as reverse observations of the same relation. Whether this axiom holds for a specific observable is a question for the declaring party at declaration time. *Consequence:* within the declared admissibility conditions, a symmetric declared edge-image signals inadmissible structure — a reverse edge declared without satisfying the distinctness axiom and independent provenance requirement. Ring topology is inadmissible: the closing edge declares a node as its own relational predecessor through a chain; within the declared admissibility conditions, no quantity has itself as a relational predecessor, as such a quantity requires solving a fixed-point equation to have a definite value, which is not traceable to an observable through M.

**Relational evolution direction.** The ordering of the declared sequence {M(o₁), M(o₂), ..., M(oₙ)} has exactly one admissible direction — the direction the process itself moves, traceable to an observable through M. This direction is a property of the process, not of the index. Indexing the sequence 1, 2, ..., n does not declare the direction; it assigns labels. The direction is declared when Origin identifies what observable property of the process establishes that oₖ₊₁ follows oₖ — for example: an oscillating system moves from floor through expression to recovery, establishing one declared process step in one direction; energy flows from a higher-relational-contrast configuration toward a lower one; a conformational change proceeds from one declared state toward another; a decaying system moves irreversibly from one configuration toward its terminal state. These are all declared process steps. None require recurrence or periodicity. Temporal ordering is not a primitive declaration — it is a downstream projection of relational evolution onto a one-dimensional axis. Time does not establish direction; the process establishes direction, and time is one way of labeling the steps of that process after the direction has been identified from the observable. *Constraint:* the direction of relational evolution must be declared by identifying the observable property of the process that establishes which observation is prior and which is current. Index order alone is not an admissible direction declaration. A declared direction of relational evolution that cannot be traced to an observable property of the process is fabricated within D.

**Composition.** V6 defines two kernels. *Primary kernel* (V6, new): E_primary(x) = Σ(Δ(x)) over spatial relations at the Primary Region — where persistence is not yet confirmed and B is not active. *ABR kernel* (V5, unchanged): Phase 1 produces E_spatial = R(B(A(x)), ρ(A(x))) over spatial relations; Phase 2 produces A_persistence → B_persistence → R_persistence over persistence relations using the same operator formulas over edge-valued loci. The order A → B → R is fixed in both phases. C is a declared projection, not a kernel operator. B activates when persistence is confirmed (ρ_P → 1, open condition); until then, the primary kernel is the correct kernel.

**A — relational gradient.** Spatial form: A(x)[e] = x[s] − x[t] over each declared spatial edge e = (s, t). Persistence form: A_persistence[e] = E_current[e] − E_prior[e] over each declared persistence relation — the directed difference between the current and prior complete kernel output at edge e. The formula is identical; the loci are edge-valued rather than node-valued. The direction E_current − E_prior is fixed by the observable: E_prior is the state before the declared relational step, E_current is the state after it. The reverse direction is not traceable to an observable through M. *Constraint:* take the directed difference and nothing else — add no relation and no direction the declaration did not trace to an observable through M. This holds for both forms. *Sequential constraint on persistence form:* A_persistence is admissible only when E_prior is the kernel output from a prior declared observation in the sequence {M(o₁), M(o₂), ...}. A_persistence computed against a zero prior (cold start) is admissible on the first declared step; thereafter, E_prior must come from the preceding actual observation, not from a model, a parameter value, or a repeated static configuration. A_persistence over a static geometry — where E_current = E_prior because M(o) does not change — produces zero output. This is the correct declared result, not a failure. It states that no relational evolution occurred across that declared step.

**Δ — primary directed difference.** Δ is A under a declared renaming, identifying its role as the irreducible primitive of the primary kernel. Δ(x)[e] = x[s] − x[t] over each declared spatial edge. Formula identical to A. Used in the primary kernel where B is not active.

**B — local accumulation.** B(g)[e] = g[e] + Σ_{f ∈ succ(e)} g[f], same direction only. *Constraint:* accumulate only along declared continuation. A terminal edge accumulates nothing; no boundary is closed to supply one. B is absent from the primary kernel — it is not instantiated as an identity operator. B activates when persistence is confirmed.

**R — antisymmetric circulation.** R(g)[e] = g[e] + ρ[e]·( Σ_{succ(e)} g − Σ_{pred(e)} g ); where spatial and component relations cross, the asymmetry of each enters the other. *Constraint:* couple by the asymmetry present. Under the declared admissibility conditions, every admissible declared structure is asymmetric — symmetry requires independent provenance per direction under the distinctness axiom, in which case both directions are distinct relations, not a symmetric pair. Do not assume the two directions equal without declared independent provenance for each.

**Σ — primary antisymmetric circulation.** Σ is R applied directly to Δ(x) without prior B accumulation. Σ(g)[e] = g[e] + ρ[e]·( Σ_{adj⁺(e)} g − Σ_{adj⁻(e)} g ), where g = Δ(x). adj⁺(e): declared edges whose source = target of e. adj⁻(e): declared edges whose target = source of e. Adjacency sums are over immediate neighbors only. Σ is R at the Primary Region limit — the same formula, the same operator, a different input condition. Not a new operator.

**ρ — per-node strength.** ρ[i] = ρ_base · m[i] / (1 + m[i]), with m[i] the largest gradient at i. *Constraint:* derived per node from A(x) or Δ(x); no aggregation beyond the node. ρ ∈ [0, ρ_base) for all declared edges.

**C — declared projection.** Any reduction of the edge field — to one value per node, to a bound, to σ². *Constraint:* state what it preserves and what it discards. No silent reduction. C is not a kernel operator in either kernel.

**Before A / Before Δ.** *Constraint:* no transformation that alters pairwise differences precedes A or Δ unless declared within M. Admissible: uniform shift, declared unit scale. All else requires declaration with preserved and discarded invariants stated.

**Primary Region invariants.** Three quantities characterize the Primary Region: rank(Im Δ) — dimension of the span of declared edge vectors in the Δ output field; rank(Im Σ) — dimension of the span of declared edge vectors in the Σ output field; |{e : adj⁺(e) ≠ ∅}| — propagation capacity. ρ_P = rank(Im Σ) / propagation capacity. Primary Region: ρ_P ≪ 1. Atomic/Molecular transition: ρ_P ≈ 1 (B activates). The transition threshold is not yet derived as a computable criterion — open condition.

*The notation says what the operators are; the constraints say what may not be added. The admissibility condition says what every quantity must be traceable to. The relational direction condition says that every declared relation has exactly one admissible direction. Bounded over D. No claim beyond D.*
